What it does

Compass

From the phone's sensors, with a reading-quality indicator.

Map AR

Orient your paper map with the camera.

Bearings

Between two points on a photo of the map.

Academy

Courses, lessons, quizzes and exercises.

Orienta AR is a learning and practice tool, not a safety or emergency navigation device. Always carry a map and compass.
