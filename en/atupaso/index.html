What it does

Your lists, your way

Create your lists, arrange the items as you like and choose how to see your progress. Check, uncheck and reuse your lists whenever you like.

One thing at a time

With “Step by step” you see one item at a time, and the list remembers where you were.

When starting is hard

“Help me start”: a short tip, one at a time, when you ask for it. And example lists you can add, change or ignore.

Always at hand

Put a list on your home screen as a shortcut, or as a widget to check items without opening the app. Save for later the lists you only use now and then.

Come back whenever you like

No streaks, no overdue tasks and nothing to justify. No reminders or notifications: nothing moves or unchecks by itself.

Private and offline

Works without Internet, with no account, no ads and no analytics. Your lists are stored on your phone and nawqira receives no data.
